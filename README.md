# Guangying Zhizao (光影智造)

Guangying Zhizao is a browser-based AI creation platform for short dramas,
comic dramas, AI images and AI video (10-90 seconds), plus AI photo
restoration and one-click AI content analysis.

- Website: https://www.rgzncz.cn
- English: https://www.rgzncz.cn/en
- FAQ: https://www.rgzncz.cn/en/faq
- Tutorial: https://www.rgzncz.cn/en/tutorial
- Work example: https://www.rgzncz.cn/case-yunhai-tiangong

## Features
AI short drama / comic drama / promotional video / film (10-90s), AI image
generation, AI photo restoration, one-click AI content analysis, and a
director & actor studio for consistent shots.

## How it works
Sign in at https://www.rgzncz.cn, choose a creation mode, describe your idea
or upload a reference, generate, then preview and download.

## Notes
Web only: no mini program, no standalone app, no AI music. AI generation is
billed in credits; there is no daily free quota, no signup bonus and no
check-in points. Users may bring their own API key or use the free
Pollinations image option.
